About Inqaba Secure Solutions

Providing security solutions with integrity, vigilance, and professionalism across KwaDukuza and beyond.

Who We Are

Inqaba Secure Solutions is a proudly South African security company founded on the values of reliability, community, and service excellence. We provide a range of services — from physical guarding to advanced alarm and surveillance systems — to ensure the safety of individuals, businesses, and neighborhoods.

Our Guiding Principles

Our Mission

To deliver peace of mind through comprehensive, tech-integrated security services tailored to meet the evolving needs of our clients.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted security brand in Southern Africa, leading in innovation, client satisfaction, and community empowerment.

Our Core Values

  • Integrity: Upholding honesty and strong moral principles in all dealings.
  • Vigilance: Maintaining constant readiness and watchful observation.
  • Excellence: Striving for the highest quality in every service we provide.
  • Innovation: Continuously exploring new technologies and methods.
  • Community-First: Prioritizing the safety and well-being of the communities we serve.
  • Professionalism: Conducting ourselves with the highest standards of conduct and expertise.
